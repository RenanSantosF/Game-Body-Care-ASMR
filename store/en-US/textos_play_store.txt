=== APP NAME (up to 30 characters) ===
Gross to Glow: Gross Clinic

=== SHORT DESCRIPTION (up to 80 characters) ===
Squeeze, clean and heal the grossest cases. Go from gross to glow!

=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Welcome to the grossest (and most satisfying) clinic on your phone! 🤢✨

In Gross to Glow, you are the doctor. Patients arrive with gross skin problems, and you treat them one by one until they leave clean and glowing.

🩺 HOW TO PLAY
• Drag your finger to aim the tool at the problem
• Hold the green button to use the tool
• Treat every problem in the level and see the result

🧰 TOOLS
• Needle to pop pimples and blisters
• Extractor to squeeze out pus and blackheads
• Tweezers to pull out splinters, hairs, larvae, worms and lice
• Scalpel and nail clipper for ingrown nails
• Scraper, nail file and razor for calluses, nails and beards
• Wet cloth and cotton to clean, ice spray for pain, ointment to soothe and bandages to cover

🤢 GROSS PROBLEMS
• Pus-filled pimples and blackheads
• Giant boils
• Ingrown nails and calluses
• Splinters and glass shards
• Worms and larvae under the skin
• Lice, huge beards and dirty hair
• Burns with blisters and much more: over 30 types!

😖 WATCH OUT FOR THE WRONG TOOL
Use the wrong tool and the patient feels pain and the problem gets worse. Then you'll have to clean it, apply ointment and put on a bandage. Too much pain and the patient leaves!

🧍 MANY BODY PARTS
Hands, feet, back, face and head. Every 5 levels a boss patient arrives with lots of problems at once.

✨ BEFORE AND AFTER
At the end of each level, drag the slider to compare before and after. Share the result with your friends!

⭐ LEVEL UP
Earn stars and coins, upgrade your tools and unlock new levels.

🎧 RELAXING
Squishy sounds, vibration when you squeeze and calm music. A light, quick and easy game.

Note: this game shows gross skin-care scenes (pus, blood, larvae) in a cartoon style. It is not a medical app and does not replace a health professional.

Available in English, Spanish and Portuguese.
